XIAN Yaolong

I am a doctoral researcher at Peace Research Institute Frankfurt (PRIF). My work focuses on rebel governance, conflict economies, and political legitimacy.

My research has been published in the Journal of Contemporary China, Media, War & Conflict, and The British Journal of Politics and International Relations, among others.

I hold a joint master’s degree in International Humanitarian Action from Ruhr University Bochum and Uppsala University, which I completed as an Erasmus Mundus scholarship recipient.

I am currently seeking postdoctoral research opportunities.
